# Alphabet of Basic Forms

Each letter gives seven words, one per plane, every word beginning with that letter. The word is the letter's meaning as it appears on that plane. Below the words sits the form the letter is built from, and the Hegemony coordinate.

This is the pre-bigram table. From basic form up to Action-Effect.

---

## The planes

1 Who, Metaphysical, will and direction. The agent.
2 What, Possible, faith and probability. The potential.
3 Where, Physical, matter and distance. The place or thing.
4 Why, Lyrical, meaning and resonance. The purpose.
5 How, Logical, count and consistency. The mechanism, given as the active form.
6 Cause, Historical, sequence and causality. The prior.
7 Effect, Emotive, passion and consequence. The felt result.

---

## The principle

A letter's meaning is read from its shape, and the same meaning refracts differently on each plane, so each plane takes its own word. A is activity: two streams spaced apart converging, with the bar marking activity over a span of time, potential converging to a point and expanding from one. On the Who plane that is Agent. On the Physical plane it is Apex. On the Emotive plane it is Arrival.

Letters are built from letters. B is two D's stacked and opposing on the wall of I. D is C closed against I. I and C are the two irreducibles; I rotated gives the bar and the stream, C rotated gives the four bowls.

The Hegemony coordinate is recalculated from the Actualism document on the correct axes. That document scored 0 to 1, so nothing could reach the negative half of either axis and every letter landed in the productive quadrant. On −2 to +2 they spread.

## How this feeds Action-Effect

A letter gives seven words and a coordinate. A bigram is two letters adjacent, so it gives seven word-pairs, one per plane, before any citation is consulted, plus a compound coordinate. A word is a chain of bigrams, and its Action-Effect reading assembles plane by plane from the letter words.

The Omni-Weave bigram table is a separate instrument. Its meanings are attested across languages rather than read from shape. Where a bigram's form-derived plane words and its citation-derived meanings agree, that is two methods converging. Where they disagree, both are held.

---

## Open

The plane words are first-pass. Each is testable on two conditions: it begins with the letter, and its sense is what the letter does on that plane. A better word meeting both replaces it.

X takes its words from *ex*, its spoken name, since the written X yields almost nothing. Y's Lyrical word is Why, which is the plane's own name, so the letter sits on its own plane. Both are noted rather than hidden.

The six anchors are A activity, B boundary, C container, D direction, E energy, I ice. The other twenty letters are proposed.
