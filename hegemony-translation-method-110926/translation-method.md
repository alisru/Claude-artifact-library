# Cross-Tradition Translation Method (Hegemony)

## 1. The translation object

Decompose any passage across the seven planes:

Q1 Who / Metaphysical
Q2 What / Possible
Q3 Where / Physical
Q4 Why / Lyrical
Q5 How / Logical
Q6 Cause / Historical
Q7 Effect / Emotive

Run Q2 through Q7 first. Q1 is never declared upfront, it emerges last, from what the action, mechanism, cause and effect structure requires an agent to be. Each plane recurses, Q_i to q_j to c_k.

## 2. Relations first, not words

The primitive unit of translation is never word-to-word. It is expression-to-relation-structure. Before asking what a passage corresponds to anywhere else, state what it says as a bare relation: who or what originates, acts on, or produces what, under what condition. Only after that relation is stated plainly does cross-tradition comparison begin. This is what stops "dharma = righteousness" or "Tao = God" from being smuggled in as an assumption before any structural check has happened.

## 3. Two layers per passage

Every passage carries two distinct structures, not one:

- Semantic structure: the Q.q.c coordinate, what is being said
- Transformational structure: state0 to action to state1 to trajectory, what transformation the passage is trying to produce

A command like "love your enemy" is not a proposition to file at a coordinate. It is a transformation rule: hostility to non-reciprocal response to possible interruption of hostility. Compare traditions on both layers, not just the first.

## 4. Four-way correspondence

For any two expressions being compared, score four independent channels separately, never averaged into one number:

- Lexical: do the words or labels match
- Relational: does who-acts-on-what-under-what-condition match
- Functional: does the mechanism match, same How, same Cause to Effect chain
- Trajectory: does the direction of movement match (section 6)

A pair can score low lexical and high on the other three. That is a genuine correspondence, not a coincidence of vocabulary, and must be reported as four separate scores.

## 5. Falsifiability gate

Every object is potentially relationally translatable, but not every proposed relation is structurally valid. Before grading a correspondence, run the checklist: does Who match, does What match, does Why match, does How match, does Cause match, does Effect match, does the trajectory match. A correspondence that only holds by forcing an interpretation on two or three planes does not clear the gate, it gets reported as weak or rejected, not smoothed into a high grade. This is what stops the method from becoming a universal solvent that can "prove" any two texts related.

## 6. Grading the match

Correspondences that clear the gate get graded with TBE Xi TS notation:

`=` frame-bound consistency, holds within one Q-plane only
`==` a single cross-plane verification event, confirmed by a second independent plane
`===` resonance across all seven planes simultaneously, no residue, truth proper

This grade is the confidence measure. No separate numeric confidence score is needed alongside it.

## 7. Trajectory as frame transitions

A transformation chain, state0 to action to state1, is a sequence of frame-collapse events, not a smooth arrow. Apply RPN: 1n = 0(n+1), the unit of the departure frame becomes the zero-point of the arrival frame. Mark each transition as its own event.

Whenever a passage's surface polarity and its trajectory diverge, something that reads as loss but trends toward Greater Good, or reads as gain but trends toward Greater Evil, flag it as Perceptual Inversion and re-run its (upsilon, psi) coordinate against the four corner attractors, Greater Good (+u,+p), Greatest Lie (-u,+p), Greater Evil (-u,-p), Lesser Good (+u,-p), rather than scoring the surface reading at face value.

## 8. Invariant extraction (three or more traditions)

When comparing more than a pair, do not intersect the literal wording, that discards everything distinctive until only bland common-denominator morality remains. Instead extract the invariant: the relational and transformational structure preserved across all of them, while each tradition keeps its own explanation of why that structure holds. Universal structure is not the same claim as universal vocabulary, the invariant is the former only. Example: hatred to hatred to more hatred, evil to retaliation to greater evil, attachment to reaction to suffering, all reduce to the invariant self-reinforcing negative feedback, without asserting the four traditions mean the same thing by it.

## 9. Round-trip audit

Every translation gets tested in both directions. Encode the source into the Hegemony, then reconstruct back toward the source tradition's own terms, and compare the reconstruction against the original: x, encode, Hx, reconstruct, x-hat. The test is not whether x-hat reproduces x word for word, it will not, but how much of the intended relational and transformational structure survived. This is a check on the whole encoding, distinct from remainder tagging on a single one-directional mapping (section 10), and it is what catches a translation scheme that looks fine forward but silently distorts on the way back.

## 10. Remainder

Whatever doesn't survive a translation, whether caught by the round-trip audit or by a direct source-to-target mapping, gets tagged as one of two things, never as a bare loss percentage:

- Distorted: partially transmitted, present but warped
- Blocked: did not cross at all, no counterpart exists in the target's structure

Distorted means re-examine the mapping. Blocked means accept the non-equivalence, it is data, not failure.

## 11. Provenance stays off the page

During analysis, keep a working trail: source statement, interpretation, inference, translation. That trail never appears in the rendered output. The finished document states the result.

## 12. Output format

SOURCE: tradition, passage or concept, Q.q.c coordinate, transformational structure
TARGET: tradition, passage or concept, Q.q.c coordinate, transformational structure
CORRESPONDENCE: lexical, relational, functional, trajectory (scored separately)
FALSIFIABILITY: which planes matched, gate passed or failed
GRADE: = / == / ===
TRAJECTORY: frame transitions, Perceptual Inversion flagged if applicable
INVARIANT: (only when three or more traditions are being compared)
DIVERGENCE: what differs even where the grade holds
REMAINDER: distorted (what) / blocked (what)
RESULT: one-line verdict

## 13. The pipeline in order

Text, state the bare relation (section 2), run Q2 through Q7, let Q1 emerge, semantic and transformational structure (section 3), four-way correspondence against the target, falsifiability gate, grade with TBE Xi TS, RPN frame-transition trajectory and Perceptual Inversion check, invariant extraction if three or more traditions, round-trip audit, tag remainder as distorted or blocked, render only the section 12 output block.

## Appendix: Worked example

SOURCE: Christianity, Romans 11:36, "from him, through him and to him are all things." Q.q.c: Q2 all things, origin and destination held by one agent. Q3 cosmic, not local. Q4 doxological, closing an argument on God's inscrutable judgment. Q5 three-part mechanism, from / through / to, agent as origin, means, and end simultaneously. Q6 climax of Romans 11:33-35. Q7 praise, submission. Q1 emerges: an agent standing in a three-fold origin/means/end relation to everything. Transformational structure: hearer uncertain of God's ways, to declaration of total origin/means/end, to hearer moved to doxology.

TARGET: Hinduism, Bhagavad Gita 10.8, "I am the source of all; from Me everything flows." Q.q.c: Q2 all activity and beings. Q3 cosmic. Q4 self-revelation so Arjuna worships correctly. Q5 two-part mechanism, source and flow, origin only, not also means or end. Q6 Krishna's discourse on his own manifestations. Q7 devotion. Q1 emerges: an agent as originating source of activity. Transformational structure: Arjuna uncertain of Krishna's nature, to self-declaration as source, to Arjuna moved toward devotion.

CORRESPONDENCE: Lexical low, no shared words. Relational high, both are agent-as-origin-of-all-things. Functional partial, Romans' How is three-part, Gita's is two-part, a real mismatch localized to Q5. Trajectory high, both move the hearer toward worship.

FALSIFIABILITY: Who pass. What pass. Why pass. How fail. Cause weak, different supporting arguments. Effect pass. Trajectory pass. Five of seven clear cleanly. Gate passed, not on all planes.

GRADE: ==. Confirmed on Who/What/Why/Effect/Trajectory, not ===, because How fails and Cause is weak, so there is residue.

TRAJECTORY: Single frame-collapse in each text, the hearer's frame of "one candidate object of devotion among others" collapses to "sole, total origin." No Perceptual Inversion, both read positive on the surface and trend toward the same coordinate region.

INVARIANT: Not applicable, only two traditions compared here.

DIVERGENCE: Romans' "through him" makes a means/vehicle claim, not just an origin claim. Gita 10.8 makes no such claim, it is silent on mechanism beyond source.

REMAINDER: Distorted, not blocked. The instrumental "through him" component has no counterpart in this specific verse, but the Gita elsewhere, 9.4-9.6, carries a sustaining/pervading doctrine, so the concept exists in the tradition, just not in this passage.

RESULT: Real == correspondence. Both declare a single agent as absolute origin of all things, driving the hearer toward worship. Romans additionally claims that agent as active means, a claim this Gita verse does not make.
