# Alphabet of Basic Forms — Hegemony Mapping

Each majuscule letter is placed on the Hegemony as a fontless carved form filling the ±2 square wall to wall. Its coordinate is where its ink balances. Each letter is read in four rotations, 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° counter-clockwise, and each rotation gives its own coordinate.

The summary plot of every letter at 0° is `hegemony-letters.png`. One image per letter, showing the letter placed on the grid with all four rotation points marked, is in `letters/`.

---

## Method

The letter is drawn as plain strokes at carved-capital proportions, scaled so its extremities touch the walls of the square. The stem of E stands on the +2υ wall and its bars reach the −2υ wall. The coordinate is the balance point of the ink, area-weighted, read on the diagram's axes: +υ to the left, −υ to the right, +ψ up, −ψ down. The letter is then rotated a quarter turn at a time within the same square and the balance point read again.

No per-letter judgement enters. The shape and the square decide.

## What the placement shows

**F sits in the productive quadrant at (+0.58, +0.58).** Its stem stands on the everyone wall and its two bars hang in the upper half, so its weight is up and left. F is the clearest productive form in the set at 0°.

**E, by contrast, is at (+0.41, +0.01).** Adding the base bar to F pulls it down onto the axis. Energy is level; force is lifted.

**L is the furthest from centre of any letter, at (+0.83, −0.82).** Stem on the everyone wall, bar along the floor. Lesser Good, the Good Lie. T is its vertical opposite at (0.00, +0.81), stem central, bar along the ceiling: Optimism.

**P at (+0.45, +0.64) and R at (+0.22, +0.30).** The leg drags R back toward centre. Potential held high sits further into the productive quadrant than potential released.

**J is the only base form to land in the regressive quadrant, at (−0.27, −0.54).** The stem sits right of centre and the hook drops below.

**Nine letters sit at or within a few hundredths of the origin: H, I, N, O, S, X, Z, plus G and V, W within a tenth.** These are the forms whose ink is symmetrical about the centre. The Hegemony reads them as No-one, neutral will, in every rotation.

**Rotation moves a letter round the origin.** A quarter turn maps (υ, ψ) to (ψ, −υ), so each letter's four points lie on a circle of fixed radius. The radius is what the letter has and the rotation is where it puts it. L's radius is the largest, about 1.17; the origin letters have none. C at 0° is (+0.44, 0) and at 270° is (0, +0.44): the open bowl faces everyone, then faces up.

## Process reading: υ over t

The balance point above is the 2D projection, ο. What the projection discards is the letter's progression along the depth axis, t. Following the Hellenic Ideogrammatic System, ο = Proj_x(υ): the static shadow is what remains once the process has been looked at end-on, and a symmetrical form casts a shadow at the origin whatever it did on the way.

To recover the process, each letter is traced as a path over t in its standard left-to-right reading order and the path is followed across the grid. One image per letter, path coloured blue at t = 0 through to red at t = 1, is in `process/`.

Three quantities come out of the trace. **Start** and **end** are where the path enters and leaves the grid. **Net** is end minus start, the first-order content of the process. **Winding** is the signed sweep of the path about the origin, positive anticlockwise on the diagram; its sign is the handedness of the spiral and its magnitude is how much of the grid the process encloses.

One thing to hold: because page-right is −υ, a path drawn anticlockwise on the page reads clockwise on the diagram. O traced the way the Hellenic document gives it, cos then sin, comes out with negative winding here. The handedness flips at the projection and the flip is the diagram's, not the letter's.

### The nine at the origin, decoded

**I.** Static (0, 0). Process: enters at (0, +1.68), leaves at (0, −1.68), net (0, −3.36), winding zero. A pure descent along the will axis with no turn. The wall is a fall from optimism to pessimism that never leaves the centre line.

**O.** Static (0, 0). Net zero: it ends where it began. Winding −8.86, the largest sweep in the set. O has no displacement and the most rotation. It is the only letter whose whole content is turning, which is the spiral seen end-on exactly as the Hellenic document has it.

**H.** Static (0, 0). Net (−3.36, −1.68). Winding +1.41. Enters at the everyone-optimism corner, leaves at the me wall on the axis. Two descents and a crossing. Hold is a process that starts in the productive corner and finishes at the self.

**N.** Static (0, 0). Net (−3.36, +3.36), winding zero. Enters at everyone-pessimism, leaves at me-optimism. The single crossing diagonal carries the whole path from the constructive corner to the reductive one with no turn at all. Next is a straight transfer across the grid.

**S.** Static (0, 0). Net (+1.68, −2.00), winding zero. Two arcs of opposite sense that cancel exactly, so the sweep is nothing, but the path still moves from the upper-me side to the lower-everyone side. Sequence turns twice and ends up nowhere in rotation, somewhere in position.

**X.** Static (0, 0). Net (0, −3.36), winding −2.82. Two crossings, each from top to bottom, so the net is a pure descent like I, but with rotation that I lacks.

**Z.** Static (0, 0). Net (−3.36, −3.36), winding zero. Everyone-optimism to me-pessimism, the full diagonal, no turn.

**V and W.** Static near (0, 0). Both net (−3.36, 0), winding −2.82. Enter and leave on the optimism line, everyone side to me side, having dipped through the bottom. W dips twice and the winding does not double, because the two dips cancel each other's turn.

**G.** Static (0.03, −0.06). Net (+1.09, −1.08), winding −8.04, nearly O's. Gather is O's rotation with a small displacement added by the inward bar and stub.

### The other seventeen, recoded

The static point stays as the letter's standing position. The process adds where it travels. The recoding is the pair together: what the letter is, and what it does.

**E and F.** Same start, everyone-optimism corner. E's path ends at me-pessimism, net (−3.36, −3.36); F's ends at (−1.40, 0), net (−3.08, −1.68). Energy runs the full diagonal to the far corner; force stops on the axis. The static reading had E level and F lifted. The process reading has E going further and lower.

**L.** Static (+0.83, −0.82), the furthest displaced form. Net (−3.36, −3.36), winding −5.64. Level is the one letter that both sits in a corner and sweeps the whole diagonal, with a large clockwise turn at its foot.

**T.** Static (0, +0.81). Net (−1.68, −3.36). Time stands at optimism and runs down the centre to pessimism.

**C, D, B.** All three start at the top and end at the bottom on the same side, net (0, −2.16), (0, −3.36), (0, −3.52). Static: C (+0.44), D (+0.31), B (+0.17), walking inward. Process: the descent lengthens as the bowl is closed and doubled. Containment stands nearer the centre and falls further.

**P and R.** P net (0, −1.52), a short descent ending at the axis with the bowl unfinished below. R net (−3.36, −3.36), the leg carrying it to the far corner. Potential stops; release runs.

**A.** Static (0, −0.12). Net (−2.80, +1.28), winding +3.65. Activity is the only letter whose process rises on the will axis while crossing from everyone to me. The bar at the end is what pulls it back up.

**M.** Static (0, +0.20). Net (−3.36, 0). Enters and leaves on the pessimism line, having climbed to optimism twice. The inverse of V.

**Q.** O's rotation, −8.45, with the tail adding net (−1.68, −3.36). Query is the sealed cycle plus a drop to the regressive corner.

**J.** Static (−0.27, −0.54), the only base form in the regressive quadrant. Net (+2.00, −2.48), winding +3.36: it enters on the me side and hooks back toward everyone as it falls.

**K.** Net (−3.36, −3.36), winding −2.82. Static (+0.44, 0). Key stands with C and runs with E.

**U.** Static (0, −0.31). Net (−3.36, 0), winding −7.25. Enters and leaves on the optimism line like V, but sweeps far more, because the bowl is a turn where V is a corner.

**Y.** Net (−1.68, −3.36), winding zero. Enters at everyone-optimism, leaves at bottom-centre, no turn. Yield converges and drops.
