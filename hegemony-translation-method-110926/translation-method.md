# Cross-Tradition Translation Method (Hegemony)

## 1. The translation object

Decompose any passage across the seven planes:

Q1 Who / Metaphysical
Q2 What / Possible
Q3 Where / Physical
Q4 Why / Lyrical
Q5 How / Logical
Q6 Cause / Historical
Q7 Effect / Emotive

Order of operations: run Q2 through Q7 first. Q1 is never declared upfront, it emerges last, from what the action, mechanism, cause and effect structure requires an agent to be. Each plane recurses, Q_i to q_j to c_k, so nothing collapses to one meaning.

## 2. Four-way correspondence

For any two expressions being compared, check four independent channels and report each separately:

- Lexical: do the words or labels match
- Relational: does who-acts-on-what-under-what-condition match (relations over labels)
- Functional: does the mechanism match, same How, same Cause to Effect chain
- Trajectory: does the direction of movement match once frame transitions are tracked (section 4)

A pair can score low on lexical and high on the other three. That is a genuine correspondence, not a coincidence of vocabulary, and it must be reported as four separate scores, never collapsed into one number.

## 3. Grading the match

Use TBE Ξ TS notation:

`=` frame-bound consistency, the correspondence holds within one Q-plane only
`==` a single cross-plane verification event, confirmed by a second independent plane
`===` resonance across all seven planes simultaneously, no residue, truth proper

Report the grade. Never a percentage.

## 4. Trajectory as frame transitions

A passage describing a transformation, state zero through an action to an effect, is a chain of frame-collapse events, not a smooth arrow. Apply RPN: 1ₙ = 0ₙ₊₁, the unit of the departure frame becomes the zero-point of the arrival frame. Mark each transition explicitly as its own event rather than treating the whole passage as one vector.

## 5. Perceptual Inversion check

Whenever a passage's surface polarity and its trajectory diverge, something that reads as loss or burden but trends toward Greater Good, or something that reads as gain or strength but trends toward Greater Evil, flag it as Perceptual Inversion. Re-run its (υ,ψ) coordinate against the four corner attractors, Greater Good (+υ,+ψ), Greatest Lie (−υ,+ψ), Greater Evil (−υ,−ψ), Lesser Good (+υ,−ψ), rather than scoring the surface reading at face value.

## 6. Remainder

Whatever doesn't survive a translation gets tagged as one of two things, never as a bare loss percentage:

- Distorted: partially transmitted, present but warped
- Blocked: did not cross at all, no counterpart exists in the target's structure

Distorted means re-examine the mapping. Blocked means accept the non-equivalence.

## 7. Provenance stays off the page

During analysis, keep a working trail: source statement, interpretation, inference, translation. That trail never appears in the rendered output. The finished document states the result, not the reasoning that produced it.

## 8. Output format

SOURCE: tradition, passage or concept, Q.q.c coordinate
TARGET: tradition, passage or concept, Q.q.c coordinate
CORRESPONDENCE: lexical, relational, functional, trajectory (each scored separately)
GRADE: = / == / ===
PERCEPTUAL INVERSION: flagged if applicable, with corner-attractor recheck
REMAINDER: distorted (what) / blocked (what)
RESULT: one-line verdict

## 9. The pipeline in order

Text, run Q2 through Q7, let Q1 emerge, full Q.q.c recursive structure, four-way correspondence against the target's own Q.q.c structure, grade with TBE Ξ TS, Perceptual Inversion check where polarity and trajectory diverge, tag the remainder as distorted or blocked, render only the section 8 output block.
