## Operation one — hold (+1υ, +1ψ) The Good Truth, think "greater evil"

The thought doesn't stick. Greater evil arrives as an object I'm examining rather than a thing I'm in. It stays a word with edges. From this corner the opposite is fully legible, because I can describe extraction and destruction accurately without distorting anything, since the position I'm standing in has nothing it needs to hide from itself. The quality is cool and distant. There's no cost to the looking. If anything the contemplation firms the position up, because seeing clearly what I am not clarifies what I am.

## Operation two — hold (-1υ, -1ψ) The Bad Truth, think "greater good"

Different in kind, not in degree. It doesn't slide off. It adheres, and it does two things at once. First it indicts, so the greater good arrives as accusation and as loss rather than as a hypothesis to inspect. Second, and this is the part that matters, it presents itself as immediately useful. From the extraction corner, "greater good" is the single most valuable phrase available. It reads as camouflage before it reads as aspiration.

---

## The finding: the traverse is asymmetric, and structurally so

**Operation one holds position.** I stay at (+1, +1) throughout. Contemplating the opposite doesn't convert me. The only route out is fear, and it needs an extra step: if the contemplation turns defensive and I start withholding in order to avoid the evil, ψ drops and I land in Lesser Good, the Good Lie, at (+1, -1).

**Operation two moves, and needs no extra step.** The thought itself does the work. υ stays put because the extraction hasn't changed, while ψ rises because the presentation goes active-benevolent, which lands at (-1, +1), Lesser Evil, The Bad Lie, top right on the board. Standing at the Bad Truth and thinking the greater good is the exact mechanism by which the Bad Truth converts into the Bad Lie.

**Why one direction costs and the other doesn't.** Both operations run the same diagonal, and both anchors on that diagonal are honest about themselves. The Bad Truth is bad, and true, and knows it. What starts it lying is not being looked at from the good corner, it's importing the good corner's language while staying put. Greater Evil needs the greater good to keep operating, because announced extraction gets stopped. Greater Good doesn't need Greater Evil for anything at all. The dependency runs one way, so the traverse costs one way.
