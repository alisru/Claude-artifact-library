## 1. The thesis

Anything new is subject to BCS, the best in the span of causal time.

What is new can only be made from what already is. Not roughly what was there, exactly what was there. Perfect reason, perfect cause and effect, operating on precisely the conditions present at that moment. Nothing missing, nothing wasted, nothing that could have gone another way given what was available.

"Best" here is not a grade against an ideal. There is no shortfall to grade. It is the exact output of exactly what was available.

You can only use what is from what was to make what will be.

The universe is included, because it was new once, and causality applies to it too.

## 2. The frame

A moment is not a container that a state sits inside. A moment is the state.

The unit is the Totality Event Frame:

Totality_Event_Frame[when] = [ past[when_prev], present[when_now], future[when_nextPredicted] ]

where

when = [ who[when], what[when], why[when], where[when], how[when], cause[when], effect[when] ]

The seven components are the seven planes.

1. Who, the metaphysical plane. Will and direction.
2. What, the possible plane. Faith and probability.
3. Where, the physical plane. Matter and distance.
4. Why, the lyrical plane. Meaning and resonance.
5. How, the logical plane. Count and consistency.
6. Cause, the historical plane. Sequence and causality.
7. Effect, the emotive plane. Passion and consequence.

Six of these bound what can be produced. Who does not. In the structure, Who is the emergent axis, the driver, and the remaining six form three paired axes: possible against physical, lyrical against logical, historical against emotive. A frame is the space. Will is the direction of travel through it. Making will a component of the space would put the actor inside their own option set.

### 2.1 The cycle

The three slots are one register in different completion states.

Past holds complete tuples. Present is where filling happens. Future is the tuple now being filled toward.

When the future slot is complete it becomes a past slot, and it cycles again.

This is what time is under BCS. Not a line the states are arranged along, but the repeated closure of tuples, each closed one becoming the material of the next.

### 2.2 Present is not a slot

Past and future hold tuples. Present is the operation that fills them.

The present is therefore the only position at which a tuple is incomplete and action occurs anyway. Everything done is done from there.

### 2.3 The frame contains its own successor

The future slot is filled from the present frame. So a model of what comes next sits inside the frame that produces it, and is part of what constrains what the successor becomes.

Anticipation therefore constrains action without any backward causation. What does the work is the present-tense model, not the future state.

## 3. Fill order

A frame does not close by having its components filled in any sequence.

What comes first. Then where, why, how, cause, effect. You cannot get complete information otherwise.

The reason is that the remaining six are components of something. A thing not yet picked out as a thing has no location to fill, no meaning, no mechanism, no traceable cause and no consequence. There is no referent for the other questions to be about.

Components can be gathered ahead of What. They do not hold. Gathered out of order they are misattributed, rejected, or come back empty, and the frame stays open.

Three instances.

**What filled wrongly.** Le Verrier filled What for Uranus as an unseen perturbing mass, computed Where from it, and Galle found Neptune on 23 September 1846 within one degree of the predicted position. He then filled What for Mercury as a planet he named Vulcan. The Where came out empty. Nothing was ever found there. Same method, same man, and the frame never closed, because the first component was wrong.

**How left empty.** Wegener filled What and Where for continental drift in 1912. The coastline fit, the fossil distributions and the matching geological structures were all in hand. The driving forces he proposed were calculated as orders of magnitude too weak, so How stayed empty and the frame stayed open until Hess supplied seafloor spreading in 1962.

**What left unstable.** Semmelweis filled Effect and How at the Vienna General Hospital in 1847, ordering examiners to scrub in chlorinated lime and recording the fall in maternal mortality that followed. What remained "cadaverous particles". The frame did not close until germ theory supplied it.

These are one mechanism, not three categories.

## 4. Micro-span

The frame is indexed to an actor's position, not to a date.

A span indexed to a time alone is a period, and periods are not option sets. "The span of 1838" bundles a minister in London with a stockman on a frontier run and treats them as facing one set of options. They shared no material, no operations, no information and no incentives.

People are brought up differently. Some optimise for self-benefit and some for other-benefit, usually self-benefit in self-benefit environments. Conditioning, information and sub-culture vary within any period, which is why factions exist inside every population and why nothing follows from what "the era" could have done.

So the coordinates have to be given: whose frame, at what position, open in which respects.

### 4.1 A frame has a shape, not just a size

Two frames of the same size can be closed in entirely different places. The three paired axes give three ways a frame can be lopsided.

Possible against physical. Wide in matter and narrow in individuation is holding everything needed and not knowing what one is holding. The reverse is knowing exactly what is wanted and having none of it.

Lyrical against logical. Wide in meaning and narrow in mechanism is a pattern that is legible with no account of how it works.

Historical against emotive. A long view backward with none forward, or the reverse.

## 5. Information is inside the frame

Even the information held is causally limited.

Knowledge is a made thing. It is produced from what was already present, by operations that were themselves produced. No actor can step outside its information state at the moment of acting in order to act on a better one, because acquiring information is an act, made from what is there.

Operations are made things too. To get to 1 you need to accumulate from 0, and invent 0.1, and addition. An actor holding all the material for a thing while lacking the operation that combines it is bounded as firmly as one holding no material at all.

Individuations are made things. Nothing can be aimed at until it has been picked out.

All people use the information available to them, per their conditioning and reasoning.

## 6. Proofs

**Axioms.**

A1. Every made thing is made from what already exists.

A2. Every event is fully accounted for by what preceded it.

A3. Every made thing occupies, consumes or excludes some portion of what exists.

A4. The information, operations and individuations held at a moment are themselves made things, and are subject to A1.

**Definitions.**

D1. F(a, t) is the frame of actor a at t: the seven components in their filled or unfilled state.

D2. S(a, t) is the set of things producible from F(a, t).

D3. A making is a transition from F(a, t) to some x present at a later t, where x was not present.

D4. BCS: for any made x arising from F(a, t), x is a member of S(a, t) and x is the resultant of the conditions in F(a, t).

### Theorem 1. Non-comparativity

There is no y such that x fell short of y.

Two cases. For y outside S(a, t), it was not producible from F(a, t) at all, by A1.

For y inside S(a, t), the claim requires that y was producible, was passed over, and was better. But by A4 the information state is itself the output of the prior frame, and selection proceeds on that state and no other. An actor who produced x rather than y did so because x was the better option on the information at hand. Declining an option is itself a selection made on that information, not an exemption from it. There is no member of the span that was available, better on what the actor actually held, and passed over anyway.

One can argue that a person should have done infinite other things in infinite other ways from one point in time to the next. They did not, because they were acting on and acted upon by internal and external information at that moment.

### Theorem 2. Iteration is necessary

If x requires m, and m is not in F(a, t), then x is not producible at t, and no quantity of power, knowledge or intention changes it.

m is itself a made thing with its own requirement m', and so on. So producing x requires an ordered sequence of makings, each bringing into being the material of the next.

Iteration is not the slow route to a result. It is the only route by which the material of the result comes to exist.

### Theorem 3. The perfection request

"State P should have obtained at t0" requests the suspension of A1 or A4.

If P was not in S(a, t0), it was not producible, and the request is that something be made out of what is not there.

If P was in S(a, t0) and was not produced, then by Theorem 1 it was not what looked better on the information held, and the request is that the actor have selected on an information state other than the one its own history produced.

Both are the same request. One asks for absent material, the other for absent knowledge. In neither is there a second state of affairs for the comparison to refer to.

### Theorem 4. Completion

BCS applies to closed tuples only.

Until the last component is filled, the tuple is still filling, and there is no output yet for the claim to be about.

Applied to a closed tuple, BCS says the output was the exact output of that frame and the event was necessary in the only sense causality supports. Applied to a filling tuple it says nothing, because part of what will close that tuple is what the actor is about to do.

BCS therefore cannot be used as an excuse. An excuse is offered from the present, about an act being performed. BCS does not reach the present. By the time the claim holds, the frame has closed.

All events in the past are by evidence BCS. The conditions of the previous frames set up the frames that followed, precisely.

This declares events 100 per cent necessary to have occurred the ways they did, after they happened. It does not excuse them.

### Theorem 5. Suffering

Suffering is acting with a lack of understanding. It sits in the act, not in a sensation, so it is never located in whoever it lands on. Pain lands. Suffering is done.

By A4, the information in any frame is the output of the frame before it, and is therefore partial: it holds what has been produced and nothing else. No actor holds the information that later frames will produce, because it does not yet exist to be held.

Every act therefore issues from a partial state. So suffering is not an event inside the sequence. It is a property of sequencing.

This applies symmetrically. Two parties can each be aiming at what they take to be good, each on the best information available to them, and harm is produced by both. That is the ordinary case, not the exception.

## 7. Material precedence

The universe is a multidimensional resource: a collection of whats, wheres, whys, hows, causes and effects. Each frame draws from the one before it.

**Bronze and iron.** Iron work needs sustained temperatures far above bronze work, roughly 1480 degrees against 920. The bloomery that produced usable iron depends on prepared charcoal supplying both heat and the carbon monoxide that reduces the ore, on tuyeres admitting air, and on forced draught. That practice was built by bronze working. Bronze was the exact output of its frame, and part of that output was the material of iron's frame.

**Newton and Einstein.** General relativity needed three things not present in 1687. Mercury's perihelion discrepancy was identified by Le Verrier in 1859 from timed transits running back to 1697, which required a century and a half of accumulated positional astronomy. The Michelson-Morley null result came in 1887 and required an interferometer sensitive enough to detect nothing. Maxwell's equations were in place by then. Relativity was published in 1915 into a frame that held all three. Newton was exact for what was there.

**Carnivory.** Cats cannot synthesise taurine in sufficient quantity and must take it from diet; feeding cysteinesulfinic acid as a precursor produces no significant increase in plasma, whole blood or muscle taurine. They cannot make arginine, and cannot convert beta-carotene to vitamin A. Dollo's law names the general case: once the developmental pathway underlying a complex trait is lost, it does not reappear in its original form. No choice was made. The option was lost through commitments stacked over generations, which is causality running over time.

**Technological lock-in.** The decisions available now are limited by decisions already made, even where the original conditions no longer obtain. Path dependence is the same structure described by economists.

## 8. The limit case: making from nothing

Every complaint about the world reduces to a complaint about one frame.

The universe was defined from formless infinity in the first few moments of time itself. Everything after is made from what that resolution produced. It is not one application of BCS among others. It is the frame with no predecessor, and every other frame is downstream of it.

### 8.1 Light

Take the origin frame epistemically.

God is a being of information, prior to the physical universe. At that frame, information is what is there, and by A1 whatever is made first must be made from what is there. So light is first because information is the only available material. Light is information.

A sun is a source of information other than God.

That is the whole of the ordering. First information. Then sources of it other than the origin. A secondary source cannot precede the thing it is a source of, which is Theorem 2 in its first instance.

Nothing here needs the light of day one to be photons, and nothing here is a correction applied later.

### 8.2 The uncaused term

By A1 and A3, every made thing takes from what already is. So the sequence either runs back without end, or terminates in something that makes without taking.

Only something uncaused, depending on nothing before itself, could make without taking. Everything else has to take.

Taking is therefore not a flaw that slipped into the design. It is what being a finite thing that lasts through time means. A universe where nothing took from anything would be a universe with no finite things in it. Taking is the price of anything besides the uncaused term existing as a separate thing in time at all.

### 8.3 The universe as locus

To all effect the universe acts like a wand. A locus. A focus where everyone's infinite potential and God's can be gathered and expressed in any form of order at all.

Man does the same thing through simulations, making universes inside the universe with no rules or laws except self-imposed ones, and he builds them for reasons, which are the same reasons the universe was made, because the reason is part of what is being copied.

## 9. What BCS is for

BCS is not a moral judgement and is not meant to have an effect. It is a statement on the nature of causality.

Its use is to put to rest claims of the form "the eleventh century should have had nukes."

That claim, and everything shaped like it, requires an alternative. Specifying the alternative means specifying material, operations or knowledge that were not there, which specifies a different frame, which specifies a different world. The comparison is then between two unrelated things, one of them invented.

Alternative history is good for storymaking. As argument it retards discourse, and it terminates in the same place every time, which is a critique of the being who made the universe from formless infinity in the span of a few moments.

BCS does not sort history into piles. It does not weigh what someone could have done otherwise. There is no otherwise to weigh, because the conditions that produced the act are exactly the conditions that were there.

## 10. Mistakes and regret

Mistakes can be made. Regret is impossible.

A mistake is a property of one closed frame, readable off that frame alone. Vulcan is a mistake: What was filled wrongly, so Where never filled. Establishing that needs no second frame and no alternative history. It needs the frame and its contents.

Regret is not a property. It is a relation. It sets a closed frame against a second closed frame that would have issued from the same conditions, and that second frame is exactly what does not exist. The frame produced the outcome exactly and without remainder, so any alternative requires different conditions, and different conditions are a different frame.

Regret has the grammar of a comparison and no second term.

By the structure it is stricter than that. Regret is an act performed from the present, the only position where acting occurs, and directed at a closed tuple, the only position that takes no further filling. It is work done in the one place work happens, aimed at the one place it cannot land. And by Theorem 5 it is an act from a partial state, which makes it a producer of the thing it is reacting to.

What survives is correction. Correction operates on the open tuple and takes the closed one as input, which is A1 restated: what was made becomes what the next thing is made from.

Regret treats a closed frame as though it were still fillable. Correction treats it as stock.
